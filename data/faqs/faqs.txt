         HIMACHAL HOMESTAYS — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

SECTION A — BOOKING & RESERVATIONS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How do I confirm a booking?
A: You can book directly through our AI booking agent (this chat), through
   our website, or by calling our support number at +91-98170-XXXXX. To
   confirm a booking, we need your travel dates, number of guests, preferred
   property and room type, and a WhatsApp number or email for communication.
   Once these are confirmed, you will receive a Booking Reference ID in the
   format HP-YYYYMMDD-XXXX. Our team will contact you within 2 hours on
   WhatsApp to collect the advance payment and finalise the booking.

Q: How much advance payment is required to confirm a booking?
A: We require 30% of the total stay amount as advance payment to hold the
   room. The remaining 70% is payable at the property on arrival. For
   bookings during peak season (May–June, October, December–January) or
   on long weekends and holidays, the advance is 50%.

Q: What payment methods are accepted for the advance?
A: We accept UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), NEFT/IMPS bank transfer, and
   debit/credit cards via our payment link. We do not accept cash advances.
   On-arrival payments at the property can be made in cash or UPI.

Q: Can I hold a room without paying the advance?
A: No. A room is only confirmed upon receipt of the advance payment. We
   cannot hold rooms on verbal or chat-based confirmation alone, especially
   during peak season when demand is high.

Q: How far in advance should I book?
A: For travel during peak season (May–June, Dussehra week in October,
   Christmas–New Year), we recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance.
   For the Kullu Dussehra festival specifically, properties fill 2–3 months
   ahead. Off-season bookings (November, February–March) can usually be
   made a week in advance.

Q: Can I book for the same day or next day?
A: Last-minute bookings are possible if rooms are available. Contact us
   directly on WhatsApp at +91-98170-XXXXX for real-time availability.
   Advance payment must still be made before arrival is confirmed.

Q: Is there a minimum stay requirement?
A: Most properties have a 2-night minimum during peak season and long
   weekends. Single-night bookings are available in the off-season at
   the discretion of the property.

SECTION B — CHECK-IN & CHECK-OUT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: What are the standard check-in and check-out times?
A: Check-in is from 12:00 PM (noon). Check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early
   check-in (from 9 AM) and late check-out (up to 2 PM) can be requested
   at the time of booking and are subject to availability — there is no
   extra charge if the room is free, but we cannot guarantee it.

Q: What if my bus or taxi arrives very early — before 9 AM?
A: Please inform the property in advance. Most of our host families are
   happy to store your luggage and offer you breakfast while the room is
   being prepared, even if you arrive before check-in time.

Q: What ID proof is required at check-in?
A: All guests (Indian and foreign nationals) must provide a valid government-
   issued photo ID at check-in. Accepted documents:
   - Indian nationals: Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving Licence
   - Foreign nationals: Passport (original, not photocopy)
   Couples: Both guests must carry their own individual IDs. We do not
   accept a single ID for two people.

Q: Are unmarried couples allowed?
A: Yes. We welcome all guests regardless of marital status. We ask only
   that both guests carry valid individual ID proofs as mentioned above.
   Our properties are judgement-free; we ask the same of our guests
   toward the host family and other guests.

Q: Can I check in after midnight if my journey is delayed?
A: Yes, provided you have informed the property host in advance. Late
   night arrivals must be communicated at least 3 hours before the
   expected arrival time so the host can arrange access. Do not arrive
   unannounced after 10 PM.

SECTION C — ROOMS & AMENITIES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Is WiFi available at all properties?
A: WiFi is available at all our listed properties. Speeds vary by location:
   - Manali, Kasauli, Shimla: 15–30 Mbps, generally reliable
   - Tirthan Valley, Jibhi: 5–15 Mbps, may drop during heavy rain
   - Spiti Valley (Kaza): 2–8 Mbps BSNL/Jio, can be slow or intermittent
   If you need reliable high-speed internet for remote work, we recommend
   properties in Kasauli or Manali town.

Q: Is hot water available at all times?
A: Hot water availability varies by property type and season:
   - Geyser-equipped rooms: Hot water available in specific hours (usually
     6–10 AM and 6–10 PM). The schedule is listed in each property's description.
   - Solar-heated properties (Tirthan): All-day hot water in summer, backup
     geyser on cloudy days or in winter.
   - In Spiti: Wood-fired boiler provides hot water twice daily (7–9 AM,
     7–9 PM). Water pipes can freeze below -10°C in deep winter.
   Please check the specific property description for accurate details.

Q: Are the rooms heated?
A: All our properties provide heating. Types vary:
   - Electric room heater: Available in most rooms, included in the rate
   - Fireplace: Available in Kasauli Colonial Bungalow (Oct–Feb)
   - Bukhari (traditional iron woodstove): Some Spiti and Lahaul properties
   Blankets and quilts are always provided in adequate quantity for the season.

Q: Is there mobile network coverage?
A: Coverage varies significantly by location:
   - Manali, Kasauli, Shimla, Kullu, Dharamshala: Good Jio, Airtel, Vi coverage
   - Tirthan Valley, Jibhi: Jio works; Airtel is patchy; Vi has gaps
   - Spiti Valley: BSNL works throughout; Jio works in Kaza and Tabo;
     Airtel/Vi do not work in most of Spiti. Get a BSNL SIM in Shimla if
     connectivity is important to you.
   - Inner Himalayas and trek routes: Expect no network for extended periods.

Q: Is there power backup?
A: Most properties have inverter backup for room lighting and phone charging
   during power cuts (which are common in Himachal, especially in monsoon
   and winter). High-wattage devices (hair dryer, electric kettle) may not
   run on backup power. Spiti properties have limited backup due to the
   remoteness of the grid.

Q: Can I request an extra bed?
A: Extra mattresses are available at most properties for ₹300–500/night
   per person, depending on the property. This is for children or a third
   guest in a double room. Maximum occupancy is fixed per room and cannot
   be exceeded for fire safety reasons.

SECTION D — MEALS & FOOD
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Are meals included in the room rate?
A: It depends on the property and room type. Please check the specific
   room listing. Standard inclusions across most properties:
   - Breakfast is included in almost all room rates
   - Dinner is included in most properties (MAP plan)
   - Full board (breakfast + lunch + dinner) is available at Tirthan River
     House and Spiti Stone Homestay at an additional ₹300/person/day
   Lunch is typically not included; guests are expected to eat out or
   carry packed meals on day trips.

Q: Can the host cater to vegetarian or vegan guests?
A: Yes. All our properties can provide fully vegetarian meals. Please
   mention this at the time of booking. Vegan meals (without dairy) are
   available at most properties but should be requested in advance as
   Himachali cooking uses ghee and butter extensively.

Q: What kind of food is served?
A: Host families cook home-style Himachali and North Indian food. Typical
   meals include: Rajma chawal, Roti with sabzi and dal, Seasonal vegetables,
   Chai and Paratha for breakfast. On request (with 1 day notice), hosts
   can prepare traditional specialties such as Siddu, Madra, Dham (full
   ceremonial plate), and local trout at river-side properties.

Q: Can I cook my own food?
A: Kitchen access for guests is not available at most properties, as the
   kitchen is a family space. Tirthan River House and Pine Ridge Homestay
   allow guests to use the kitchen for simple tasks (boiling water, reheating)
   with permission. Please ask the host directly.

Q: Are there restaurants nearby?
A: Yes, all our properties are within walking distance of local dhabas and
   cafes, except the Spiti Stone Homestay (where the host's full-board meals
   are the most practical option given Kaza's limited restaurant scene).

SECTION E — TRAVELLING TO HIMACHAL
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: How do I reach Manali from Delhi?
A: The most common route is the overnight Volvo AC bus from Delhi's
   Kashmere Gate ISBT or Majnu Ka Tila. Journey time is 14–16 hours;
   buses depart between 4–6 PM and arrive in Manali around 8–10 AM.
   Tickets cost ₹800–1,400 depending on operator and seat type. Book on
   HRTC website or redBus. Alternatively, fly to Bhuntar Airport (Kullu)
   from Delhi (1 hour flight, ₹3,000–8,000); Manali is 50 km from there.

Q: How do I reach Spiti Valley?
A: Two routes:
   Route 1 (from Shimla via Kinnaur): Year-round but slow — Shimla →
   Narkanda → Rampur → Rekong Peo → Nako → Tabo → Kaza. About 12–14 hours
   by bus or taxi (430 km). This route stays open in winter.
   Route 2 (from Manali via Rohtang and Kunzum Pass): Shorter (200 km,
   7–8 hours) but CLOSED every year from mid-October to late May due to
   snow. Stunning drive in summer.

Q: Do I need an inner line permit for Spiti?
A: Indian nationals do not need an inner line permit for Spiti Valley
   (including Kaza, Tabo, Key, Langza). Foreign nationals need a Protected
   Area Permit (PAP), obtainable from the District Commissioner's office
   in Kaza or Rekong Peo. Our Spiti property host can assist with this.

Q: What is the best time to visit Himachal Pradesh?
A: It depends on the destination and what you want:
   - Manali (summer, trek season): April–June, September–October
   - Manali (snow experience): December–February
   - Spiti Valley: May–October (only open season; prefer June–September)
   - Kasauli, Shimla: October–March (pleasant, less crowded)
   - Jibhi / Tirthan: March–June and September–November (best); December–
     February for snow (roads usually open)
   - Kullu Dussehra: Second week of October every year

SECTION F — SAFETY & SPECIAL NEEDS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: Are the properties safe for solo female travellers?
A: Yes. All our host families are vetted and our properties are family-run
   homes. Solo female travellers are warmly welcomed. We recommend:
   - Informing the host of your arrival time
   - Keeping our support WhatsApp number saved
   - In remote areas like Spiti, sharing your itinerary with someone at home
   Several of our most loyal guests are solo female travellers.

Q: What about altitude sickness in Spiti?
A: Spiti Valley sits at 3,500–4,500m. Altitude sickness (AMS) is a real
   risk for guests coming directly from the plains. Symptoms include
   headache, nausea, dizziness, and fatigue. We strongly advise:
   - Spend at least one acclimatisation day in Kaza before trekking
   - Drink plenty of water; avoid alcohol on the first night
   - Our Spiti host keeps Diamox (acetazolamide) and basic first-aid
     available; consult a doctor before your trip if you have concerns
   - Nearest hospital: Kaza District Hospital (0.8 km from property)

Q: Are pets allowed?
A: Pets are not permitted at any of our listed properties. This is a
   policy set by the host families and is non-negotiable.

Q: Are the properties accessible for guests with mobility limitations?
A: Our properties are traditional hillside structures with stairs and
   uneven terrain. Currently, none of our listed properties have wheelchair
   accessibility or ground-floor rooms with fully flat access. We apologise
   for this limitation and are working to add accessible properties to our
   network.
