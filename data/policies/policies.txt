         HIMACHAL HOMESTAYS — BOOKING & HOUSE POLICIES
            Last updated: January 2025
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

These policies apply to all properties listed under the
Himachal Homestays network. By completing a booking and
paying the advance, guests agree to the terms below.
Individual properties may have additional house rules
which will be communicated at the time of booking.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
POLICY 01 — CANCELLATION & REFUND POLICY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Standard Season Cancellation (Oct–Nov, Jan–Mar)
-------------------------------------------------
Cancellation 15 or more days before check-in:
  → Full refund of advance payment (100%)

Cancellation 7–14 days before check-in:
  → 50% refund of advance payment

Cancellation less than 7 days before check-in:
  → No refund. Advance payment is forfeited.

No-show (guest does not arrive without prior notice):
  → No refund. Full advance forfeited.

Peak Season Cancellation (Apr–Jun, Oct Dussehra week, Dec–Jan)
---------------------------------------------------------------
Cancellation 30 or more days before check-in:
  → Full refund of advance payment (100%)

Cancellation 15–29 days before check-in:
  → 50% refund of advance payment

Cancellation 7–14 days before check-in:
  → 25% refund of advance payment

Cancellation less than 7 days before check-in:
  → No refund. Advance payment is fully forfeited.

Force Majeure & Natural Events
-------------------------------
In the event of a verifiable force majeure situation —
road closures due to landslide, government-issued travel
advisories, or natural disasters — guests may reschedule
their booking within 6 months at no penalty. A refund
(minus payment gateway charges of 2%) is available if
rescheduling is not possible. Documentation (news link,
government advisory) may be requested.

How to Cancel
--------------
All cancellations must be communicated in writing via
WhatsApp to +91-98170-XXXXX or email to
bookings@himachalhomestays.in. Verbal cancellations are
not accepted. The date and time of the WhatsApp or email
message is taken as the official cancellation timestamp.

Refund Processing
------------------
Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 working days
to the original payment source. UPI refunds typically
arrive within 24 hours; bank transfers may take 3–5 days.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
POLICY 02 — MODIFICATION POLICY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Date Changes
-------------
Guests may request a change of check-in or check-out date
subject to availability. Requests made 7 or more days
before check-in are accommodated at no charge (once per
booking). Changes made within 7 days of check-in are
treated as a cancellation and re-booking and the standard
cancellation policy applies to the original booking.

Room Type Changes
------------------
Requests to change room type after booking are subject to
availability. If the new room is more expensive, the
difference must be paid. If the new room is less expensive,
the difference is credited toward the balance payable on
arrival (not refunded as cash for the advance portion).

Shortening Your Stay After Arrival
------------------------------------
If a guest checks out earlier than the booked check-out
date, no refund is provided for the unused nights.

Extending Your Stay After Arrival
-----------------------------------
Guests wishing to extend their stay should inform the host
at least 24 hours in advance. Extensions are subject to
availability and charged at the applicable nightly rate.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
POLICY 03 — HOUSE RULES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Quiet Hours
------------
Quiet hours are observed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM at
all properties. Guests are requested not to play loud music,
make noise in common areas, or disturb other guests or the
host family during these hours. Repeat violations may result
in the guest being asked to vacate the property without
refund.

Smoking
--------
Smoking (cigarettes, cigars, pipes, or any tobacco product)
is strictly prohibited inside all rooms and enclosed spaces.
Designated smoking areas are available outdoors at each
property. Guests who smoke inside rooms will be charged a
deep-cleaning fee of ₹2,000 and may be asked to vacate.

Alcohol
--------
Alcohol consumption is permitted at all our properties
except the Spiti Stone Homestay (where the host family
requests that guests respect local customs and the
high-altitude environment). At all other properties,
guests may consume alcohol in their rooms or designated
outdoor areas, but not in common areas or the host family's
living spaces. Disruptive behaviour related to alcohol will
result in immediate check-out without refund.

Narcotics & Illegal Substances
--------------------------------
The use, possession, or distribution of narcotics or any
illegal substance is strictly prohibited on all our
properties and their surroundings. Kasol and Parvati
Valley have a known drug culture in certain parts — we do
not condone this and our host families will not tolerate
it. Violation will result in immediate eviction and may be
reported to local police.

Visitors
---------
Guests may receive visitors during daytime hours (9 AM–8 PM)
with prior permission from the host. Overnight visitors who
are not registered guests are not permitted under any
circumstances. All guests, including visitors, must carry
valid ID.

Children
---------
Children of all ages are welcome at all our properties.
Parents are responsible for children's behaviour, especially
near rivers, open fires, and on steep hillside terrain. The
host families cannot be held responsible for accidents
arising from unsupervised children.

Pets
-----
No pets are permitted at any property. This policy has no
exceptions and will be enforced at check-in. If a guest
arrives with an undisclosed pet, the booking will be
cancelled on the spot and no refund will be issued.

Damage to Property
-------------------
Guests are responsible for any damage caused to the property,
furniture, linen, or equipment during their stay. Damage
costs will be assessed by the host and charged to the guest
before or at checkout. In the event of disputed claims, our
support team will mediate.

Plastic & Waste
----------------
We are committed to eco-responsible tourism in the Himalayas.
All our properties are single-use plastic-free zones. Guests
are requested not to bring single-use plastic bottles, bags,
or packaging onto the property. Reusable water bottles
are available for purchase or borrow at each property.
Please segregate waste (wet/dry) as the host instructs.

Photography of Host Family
----------------------------
Please ask permission before photographing host family
members, especially children. Most hosts are happy to be
photographed; please respect a refusal.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
POLICY 04 — PRICING & BILLING POLICY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Price Validity
---------------
Prices listed in the property directory and communicated
by the booking agent are valid at the time of booking.
Prices may change for future seasons; any price confirmed
at the time of advance payment is honoured regardless of
subsequent changes.

GST
----
Room tariffs are subject to GST as per Government of India
regulations:
  - Rooms below ₹1,000/night: Exempt from GST
  - Rooms ₹1,000–₹7,499/night: 12% GST
  - Rooms ₹7,500 and above/night: 18% GST
Prices quoted by the booking agent are inclusive of GST
unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Seasonal Pricing
-----------------
All properties have two pricing tiers:
  - Peak season (April–June, October, December–January)
  - Off-season (all other months)
Weekend surcharges (Friday and Saturday nights) apply at
Kasauli Colonial Bungalow only, as specified in its listing.

Billing at Checkout
---------------------
The host will provide a handwritten bill at checkout
itemising: room charges, meals (if charged separately),
extra services used (laundry, guided walks, transport).
A digital receipt can be requested via WhatsApp.


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
POLICY 05 — RESPONSIBILITY & LIABILITY
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Himachal Homestays acts as a booking platform and connects
guests with independently operated host family properties.
We are not liable for:

- Incidents arising from natural events (landslides,
  snowfall, road closures, floods, earthquakes)
- Theft of personal belongings (guests are advised to
  use in-room lockers where available)
- Health events arising from altitude sickness, food
  reactions, or pre-existing medical conditions
- Road accidents or transport delays beyond our control
- Changes in itinerary due to weather or government orders

We strongly recommend that all guests purchase comprehensive
travel insurance covering medical evacuation, trip
cancellation, and lost baggage before travelling to
remote areas of Himachal Pradesh.

Emergency Contact
------------------
Our 24-hour support line is available for genuine
emergencies (medical, safety, or accommodation crises):
  WhatsApp/Call: +91-98170-XXXXX
  Email: support@himachalhomestays.in

For medical emergencies in remote areas:
  Himachal Pradesh Emergency: 112
  Mountain Rescue (HRTC): 1800-180-8080
