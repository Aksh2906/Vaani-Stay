  QUEST  #36  ·  BHRIGU LAKE — THE SAGE'S MIRROR
▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  CLASS:    Grassland Explorer                       │
  │  LEVEL:    Beginner → Intermediate                  │
  │  XP REWARD: ████████░░  800 XP                     │
  │  DAYS:     4  (2 trek + 2 travel)                   │
  │  REGION:   Kullu Valley, above Manali               │
  │  SEASON:   May–October                              │
  │  GOLD COST: ₹8,000–₹14,000 / adventurer            │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  QUEST LORE:
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  The Bhrigu Lake Trek is often regarded as the most
  beautiful and scenic trek in Himachal Pradesh. This
  easy-to-moderate grade trek offers a memorable journey
  through lush alpine meadows, with uninterrupted views
  of snow-covered Himalayan peaks.  The lake is sacred
  as sage Bhrigu meditated here.  It is said he wrote
  the Bhrigu Samhita — the ancient astrological text —
  at this very lake. The trail leads to a serene
  glacial lake nestled at high altitude, which is
  believed to change its colour every season. 
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  ⚔ GEAR LOADOUT
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  [EQUIPPED]  Trekking shoes (not boots — trail is good)
  [EQUIPPED]  Fleece + windproof jacket
  [EQUIPPED]  Sunscreen SPF 50 (meadow UV is intense)
  [EQUIPPED]  2L water + purification tabs
  [EQUIPPED]  Camera / phone with charged battery
  [OPTIONAL]  Trekking poles (useful on descent)
  [SKIP]      Crampons — not needed May–October
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ── QUEST STAGES ────────────────────────────────────

  [ DAY 1 ]  STAGE: CHANDIGARH → MANALI
  ▸ Travel day. Overnight Volvo from Chandigarh
    (departs ~5 PM, arrives Manali ~6 AM) OR fly
    into Bhuntar and taxi to Manali.
  ▸ Rest at Manali. Vashisht hot springs in the
    afternoon. Gear check at Old Manali shops.
  ▸ MINI-BOSS: The Manali traffic jam. Patience
    required. Side quest: find the best thukpa
    café in Old Manali. Reward: hot noodles.
  SAVE POINT: Manali hotel  ₹1,500–3,000

  [ DAY 2 ]  STAGE: GULABA → BHRIGU LAKE → GULABA
  ▸ Starting from Gulaba, near Manali,  drive 22 km
    on the Rohtang road to Gulaba (3,100m). Trek
    begins here. 7 km to the lake, 400m elevation gain.
  ▸ The trail crosses open meadows — no forest cover,
    pure Himalayan grassland in every direction.
    Hanuman Tibba (5,982m) and Deo Tibba (6,001m)
    flank you north and east.
  ▸ Bhrigu Lake (4,300m): the lake is situated at
    a height of 4,300m and changes colour with
    the season  — green in summer, steel-blue in
    autumn, frozen white in winter.
  ▸ BOSS ENCOUNTER: The altitude. 4,300m without
    prior acclimatisation hits harder than expected.
    Walk slow. Breathe deep. Drink water.
  ▸ Return to Gulaba. Drive to Manali.
  SAVE POINT: Manali hotel  ₹1,500–3,000

  ★ XP BONUS: Watch sunrise from the lake (+200 XP)
    → Requires overnight camp at Bhrigu Lake
    → Adds 1 day and a cold night

  [ DAY 3 ]  STAGE: MANALI BONUS DUNGEON
  ▸ Hadimba Devi Temple (ancient carved wood boss lair)
  ▸ Roerich Gallery (culture XP, Naggar, 37 km south)
  ▸ Mall Road loot: Kullu shawls, Tibetan trinkets

  [ DAY 4 ]  STAGE: DEPARTURE
  ▸ Overnight Volvo to Delhi / fly from Bhuntar.

  QUEST COMPLETE. REWARD UNLOCKED:
  ✦ Title: "Meadow Wanderer of the Upper Kullu"
  ✦ Skill gained: High Altitude Navigation (Beginner)
  ✦ Item: 1 jar Parvati Valley wild honey

  ON THE PLATE: Manali apple juice · Gulaba roadside
  chai · Summit snack: trail mix + dark chocolate ·
  Post-trek thali at Johnson Café · Old Manali
  apple crumble to celebrate.

  QUEST  #37  ·  PATALSU PEAK — THE BEGINNER'S SUMMIT
▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  CLASS:    Peak Bagger (Entry Level)                │
  │  LEVEL:    Intermediate                             │
  │  XP REWARD: ████████████░  1,100 XP                │
  │  DAYS:     4 trek + 2 travel = 6 total             │
  │  REGION:   Solang Valley, above Manali              │
  │  SEASON:   May–June, September–October              │
  │  GOLD COST: ₹10,000–₹17,000 / adventurer           │
  └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  QUEST LORE:
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  Patalsu Peak lies at 13,451 ft above sea level
  near Manali. One of the easier trekking peaks in
  Himachal, it provides some cherished memories of the
  Himalayas. Perfect for beginners as well as true
  mountain lovers.  The peak is a non-technical
  summit — no ropes, no ice axe, no mountaineering
  experience needed. Just fitness, acclimatisation,
  and a guide. The views from the top: Rohtang Pass,
  Solang Valley, the entire Kullu horizon, and on
  clear days as far as the Spiti ranges.
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  ⚔ GEAR LOADOUT
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  [REQUIRED]  Trekking boots with ankle support
  [REQUIRED]  -10°C sleeping bag
  [REQUIRED]  Trekking poles (scree descent)
  [REQUIRED]  Warm base layers + down jacket
  [REQUIRED]  Waterproof shell (afternoon storms)
  [OPTIONAL]  Micro-spikes (early season May–June)
  [UPGRADE]   Hire a cook through your guide agency
              for hot meals at camp (+comfort XP)
  ━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  ── QUEST STAGES ────────────────────────────────────

  [ DAY 1 ]  CHANDIGARH → MANALI (overnight Volvo)
  [ DAY 2 ]  MANALI ACCLIMATISE
  ▸ Walk to Vashisht. Soak. Eat well. Sleep early.
  ▸ SIDE QUEST: Find the highest-rated momos stall
    in Old Manali. (Hint: it is down a narrow lane
    behind the main road and has no sign.)

  [ DAY 3 ]  STAGE: SOLANG → DHUNDI → PATALSU BASE
  ▸ Drive to Solang (13 km). Trek 6 km to Dhundi
    (2,900m) through pine and oak. Continue upward
    through alpine meadow to base camp (3,500m).
  ▸ Pass through the meadows of Dhundi and Bakarthach.
    Marvel at the majestic peaks of Hanuman Tibba,
    Friendship Peak, Shitidhar and Seven Sisters. 
  ▸ MINI-BOSS: The 600m elevation gain from Solang
    to base camp in one day. Slow pace wins.
  SAVE POINT: Base Camp (3,500m)  ₹ guide-arranged

  [ DAY 4 ]  STAGE: SUMMIT DAY — PATALSU PEAK 4,100m
  ▸ Leave camp at 5 AM. 3 km to summit, 600m gain.
    Rocky trail with some scrambling sections.
    No technical gear needed.
  ▸ FINAL BOSS: The last 200m. Scree, wind, and
    the altitude conspire to make every step harder
    than it looks. Keep moving. Stop and breathe.
    Repeat.
  ▸ At the summit: Rohtang Pass below you. Solang
    Valley laid out like a map. The Friendship Peak
    glacier gleaming in the morning sun.
  ▸ Descent to base camp then all the way to Solang.
  SAVE POINT: Manali hotel  ₹1,500–3,000

  [ DAY 5 ]  BONUS DUNGEON: NAGGAR CASTLE + ROERICH
  [ DAY 6 ]  DEPARTURE → DELHI

  QUEST COMPLETE. REWARD UNLOCKED:
  ✦ Title: "Summit Initiate of Patalsu"
  ✦ Skill gained: Peak Navigation (Beginner)
  ✦ Unlocks: Friendship Peak quest (need 3 more
    high-altitude treks before attempting)

  ON THE PLATE: Solang chai stall Maggi · Base camp
  cook daal and rice · Summit glucose biscuits and
  jaggery · Manali celebration thali · Old Manali
  banana pancake (legendary, widely available).

  QUEST  #38  ·  PIN PARVATI PASS — THE GRAND CROSSING
▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

  │  XP REWARD: ████████████████████  2,000 XP         │
  │  DAYS:     11 trek + 2 travel = 13 total           │
  │  REGION:   Parvati Valley (Kullu) → Pin Valley (Spiti)│

  QUEST LORE:
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  Pin Parvati Pass (5,319m) is the longest, hardest,
  and most remote trek that remains accessible to
  non-technical trekkers in all of Himachal Pradesh.
  The exhilaration of traversing a 17,457 ft pass
  is an experience of a lifetime.  It starts in the
  lush, river-loud, Israeli-café world of the Parvati
  Valley and ends in the silent brown lunar landscape
  of the Pin Valley, Spiti. Two worlds separated by
  one impossible-looking ridge. 11 days of walking
  to cross it.
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

          │ MINI-BOSS: First night above 3,800m.

          │ Pin Parvati Pass crossings fail most

  QUEST  #39  ·  MANALI–LEH HIGHWAY — THE ROAD TO LADAKH
▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓

  ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  CLASS:    Road Warrior / Highway Pilgrim           │
  │  LEVEL:    Moderate (road, not trek)                │
  │  XP REWARD: ████████████░  1,200 XP (Himachal leg) │
  │  DAYS:     5 (Himachal portion only)               │
  │  REGION:   Manali → Baralacha La → Sarchu           │
  │  SEASON:   JUNE–OCTOBER only                        │

  QUEST LORE:
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  The Manali–Leh Highway is one of the great roads on
  Earth. It crosses five mountain passes, the highest
  of which is Tanglang La at 5,328m (in Ladakh), and
  traverses landscapes that shift from green Kullu to
  brown Lahaul to lunar Spiti-adjacent to the high
  Tibetan plateau. The highway remains blocked
  completely between Darcha and Ladakh during winter
  and opens by mid-May most years.  This itinerary
  covers the Himachal Pradesh section — Manali to
  Sarchu, 250 km, five world-class landscapes.
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  ── HIGHWAY STAGE MAP ───────────────────────────────

  [ D1 ]  MANALI PREP + ATAL TUNNEL
          ▸ Fuel, supplies, cash (last ATMs at Manali).
          ▸ Drive the 8.9 km Atal Tunnel — India's
            longest road tunnel, opened 2020, connects
            Manali to Sissu in Lahaul at 3,100m.
          ▸ MINI-BOSS: The Rohtang eco-permit (if
            going over Rohtang instead of tunnel).
          SAVE POINT: Keylong  ₹900–1,800
          WAYPOINT ALT: Jispa camp (more scenic)  ₹1,500

  [ D2 ]  KEYLONG → JISPA → DARCHA → BARALACHA LA
          ▸ The Lahaul Valley road follows the Bhaga
            River north through wide brown landscape.
          ▸ Jispa (3,180m): a small camp settlement
            on the river, last place before the high
            passes. Last trees. Last chai with milk.
          ▸ Zing Zing Bar: the final chai point before
            Baralacha La. A tent. A kerosene stove.
            The best chai of your life at 4,600m.

          BARALACHA LA: 4,890m
          ▸ The pass where the Lahaul, Spiti, and
            Ladakh watersheds meet. Three rivers are

          ▸ In June: deep snow on both sides, BRO

          ▸ In August: dry, brown, and ferociously windy.
          ▸ Descend to camp at Bharatpur (4,500m).
          SAVE POINT: Bharatpur tented camp  ₹800–1,400

  [ D3 ]  BHARATPUR → SARCHU

  Lahaul is Himachal's forgotten valley. Most tourists
  drive straight through it on the way to Spiti or Leh.
  This is a mistake of the first order. Lahaul is wider,
  emptier, more dramatic, and more culturally rich than
  either destination — it simply lacks the Instagram
  shorthand that its neighbours have acquired. This
  quest treats Lahaul as the destination, not the
  route — five days dedicated entirely to the Chandra
  and Bhaga river systems, the monasteries, the
  Pattan Valley potato farms, and the high passes
  that frame the sky.
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  ── QUEST STAGES ────────────────────────────────────

  [ D1 ]  MANALI → ATAL TUNNEL → SISSU
          ▸ Cross the tunnel at dawn when the light
            on the Lahaul side is horizontal and gold.
          ▸ Sissu (3,080m): a small village famous for
            its waterfall — a thin silver thread dropping
            300m from a hanging valley directly above.
            Drive right under it on the highway.
          ▸ The Chandra River from Sissu eastward:
            wide braided channels across flat valley
            floor. Prayer flags on every ridge.
          SAVE POINT: Keylong  ₹900–1,800

  [ D2 ]  KEYLONG BASE + MONASTERY CIRCUIT
          ▸ Keylong (3,080m) is the Lahaul capital —
            unassuming, functional, and surrounded by
            monasteries. Morning: drive across the
            valley to Kardang Monastery (1 km steep
            walk above the road). Monks offer butter
            tea to genuine visitors.
          ▸ Shashur Monastery (3,940m): 2 km walk
            above Keylong through juniper forest.
            June is the annual Shashur festival —
            masked Cham dances in the monastery
            courtyard, one of Lahaul's best events.
          ▸ Afternoon: Tayul Monastery (10 km north
            of Keylong) — a large complex with a
            four-metre statue of Padmasambhava, lamps
            burning, monks chanting.
          SAVE POINT: Keylong  ₹900–1,800

  [ D3 ]  CHANDRA VALLEY → TANDI → GRAMPHOO
          ▸ Drive east from Keylong along the Chandra
            River (the Manali–Kaza highway).
          ▸ Tandi (8 km from Keylong): the sacred
            confluence of Chandra and Bhaga rivers
            — where the Chenab River is born. Flags
            everywhere. BRO signs. The last petrol
            pump before Kaza.
          ▸ Continue to Gramphoo (3,300m) — the road
            junction where the Kaza road branches
            east and the Leh road goes north.
          ▸ SHORT TREK UNLOCK: From Gramphoo, a 4 km
            walk to the moraine above gives views of
            both the Manali side and the Chandra Valley.
          ▸ Return to Keylong or camp at Gramphoo.
          SAVE POINT: Camp at Gramphoo / Keylong

  [ D4 ]  MIYAR VALLEY SIDE QUEST
          ▸ Drive from Keylong 53 km to Udaipur HP.
          ▸ En route: Trilokinath Temple — one of the
            rare temples sacred to both Hindus and
            Buddhists, a six-armed white marble deity.
          ▸ Turn into Miyar Valley: 75 km of glacial
            wilderness, Mt Menthosa (6,443m) at its
            head, no tourist infrastructure.
          ▸ Drive 20 km into the valley. Walk 4 km
            up the Miyar Nala. Return by afternoon.
          ▸ BOSS ENCOUNTER: The Miyar Valley road
            ends at a hanging glacier snout. The ice
            calves in the afternoon. Do not camp
            directly below it.
          SAVE POINT: Udaipur HP guesthouse  ₹700–1,200

  [ D5 ]  RETURN TO MANALI + ROHTANG (IF OPEN)
          ▸ Drive back through Lahaul one final time.
            Stop at every viewpoint you skipped
            on the way in. They all deserve it.
          ▸ If Rohtang is open (check eco-permit),
            cross it for the contrast view: turn and
            look north from Rohtang at the vast brown
            Lahaul spread — then turn south at the
            sudden green of Kullu. The mountain
            creates two worlds with one ridge.
          ▸ Arrive Manali by evening.
          SAVE POINT: Manali hotel  ₹1,500–3,000
