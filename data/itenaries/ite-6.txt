│           I T I N E R A R Y   N o .  1 6                │
│                                                         │
│     PIN VALLEY — THE VALLEY THAT GROWS NOTHING          │
│           AND FEEDS YOUR SOUL COMPLETELY                │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  DURATION       6 Days
  REGION         Pin Valley National Park, Spiti
  THEME          Snow Leopard country + Buddhist villages
  SEASON         June – September ONLY
  STARTS AT      Kaza  (fly Delhi → Bhuntar → bus, or
                        drive Shimla → Kinnaur → Spiti)
  DIFFICULTY     ★★★★☆  Challenging
  BUDGET         ₹16,000 – ₹28,000 / person

  ╔═══════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  THE NUMBERS                      ║
  ║  Altitude range:  3,800 – 4,900m  ║
  ║  Snow Leopard pop: ~5–8 in valley ║
  ║  Annual rainfall:  ~170mm (desert) ║
  ║  Road condition:  Unpaved beyond  ║
  ║                   Mikkim          ║
  ║  Mobile network:  None inside     ║
  ╚═══════════════════════════════════╝

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DISTANCE TABLE  (from Kaza)
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  Kaza → Sagnam        25 km   45 min
  Sagnam → Mikkim      12 km   30 min
  Mikkim → Mudh        15 km   50 min  (rough)
  Mudh → Pin-Parvati   Trek begins here
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 1  ·  KAZA → SAGNAM → MIKKIM
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Leave Kaza and cross the Spiti river to enter Pin
  Valley. The road immediately shifts from grey scree
  to a valley so wide and open you feel you have
  entered a different planet. Pin River runs grey-
  green with glacial melt. The mountains on both
  sides are layered horizontal bands of red, ochre,
  and purple sediment — a billion years of geology
  exposed in cross-section.

  Stop at Sagnam village. The Kungri Monastery here
  is one of the oldest in Spiti — Nyingma tradition,
  dark interior, yak-butter lamps, monks who speak
  to you in broken English and perfect Tibetan.

  Continue to Mikkim. Small, dusty, real. This is
  where the road gets rough.

  TONIGHT  →  Homestay in Mikkim  ₹800–1,200 with meals
  SIGNATURE DISH  →  Tsampa porridge with dried apricot

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 2  ·  MIKKIM → MUDH (ROAD END)
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  15 km of broken road through the heart of Pin
  Valley National Park. You will cross the Pin river
  twice on narrow wooden bridges that seem held
  together by optimism alone.

  Mudh village (3,810m) is the last settlement.
  Population: approximately 150. Economy: buckwheat,
  peas, and shepherding. The village is built against
  a cliff face in traditional Spitian style — flat
  roofs, prayer flags, a monastery at the top.

  Afternoon: Trek to the meadows above Mudh for the
  best snow leopard tracking territory. Your guide
  will read pugmarks, scrapes, and scent markings.
  You will almost certainly not see the leopard.
  You will absolutely feel its presence.

  Sunset from Mudh's monastery terrace: the valley
  turns blood orange, then grey, then the stars come
  out thicker than you have ever seen.

  TONIGHT  →  Homestay in Mudh  ₹1,000–1,500 with meals

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAYS 3–4  ·  WILDLIFE TRACKING + PARA GLACIER TREK
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Day 3: Early morning wildlife walk with a local
  guide — blue sheep (bharal) graze on the slopes
  above the village every morning without fail.
  Where bharal gather, snow leopards follow.
  The golden eagle and lammergeier are constant
  overhead presences.

  Afternoon: trek toward Para glacier (7 km, moderate).
  The glacier tongue is retreating visibly year on year
  — a slow, melancholy spectacle of climate change in
  real time. The ice is blue at its base.

  Day 4: Rest day in Mudh. Talk to the older villagers.
  Ask about the old trade routes to Spiti before the
  roads came. Ask about the winter — six months of
  snow, no road, complete isolation. Some families
  still choose to stay.

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 5  ·  RETURN TO KAZA + KUNGRI MONASTERY EVENING
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Drive back to Sagnam. Spend the afternoon properly
  inside Kungri Monastery this time — the head lama
  often grants audiences to genuine visitors (arrive
  respectfully, small donation appreciated). The wall
  paintings date to the 14th century and are kept
  deliberately dark to protect them.

  Evening back in Kaza. The town's tiny café scene
  will feel like a metropolis after four days in Mudh.

  TONIGHT  →  Hotel/guesthouse in Kaza  ₹1,200–2,000

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 6  ·  KAZA — GUE MUMMY + DEPARTURE
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Before leaving: 20 km south of Kaza is Gue village,
  where a 500-year-old mummified monk sits cross-legged
  in a small shrine in perfect preservation. Discovered
  after an earthquake in 1975. No explanation. Just
  a monk, still sitting, still waiting.

  Then depart Kaza toward Shimla (via Kinnaur, 2 days)
  or toward Manali (via Kunzum, 1 day if open).

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  SIDEBAR: WHAT TO KNOW ABOUT SNOW LEOPARDS       │
  │                                                  │
  │  Best sighting months: Feb–Mar (winter tracks)   │
  │  and Jun–Jul (following bharal up to high meadow)│
  │  Hire a Snow Leopard Conservation tracker from   │
  │  the Nature Conservation Foundation (NCF) office │
  │  in Kaza — they run citizen science programs     │
  │  where travellers assist in camera-trap studies. │
  │  www.ncf-india.org for details.                  │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  FOOD TRAIL: Tsampa porridge, Butter tea (po cha),
  Nettle soup (stinging nettle is a Pin Valley staple),
  Chhurpi (dried yak cheese, rock-hard, long-lasting),
  Spitian bread with apricot jam.



┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  ✦ WEEKEND ESCAPE ✦                     │
│           I T I N E R A R Y   N o .  1 7                │
│                                                         │
│    REWALSAR LAKE + MANDI'S TEMPLES — 3-DAY CIRCUIT     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  DURATION       3 Days
  REGION         Mandi + Rewalsar (Tso Pema)
  THEME          Sacred geography — where 3 religions meet
  SEASON         Year-round (avoid peak monsoon Jul–Aug)
  STARTS AT      Chandigarh or Mandi
  DIFFICULTY     ★★☆☆☆  Easy
  BUDGET         ₹6,000 – ₹11,000 / person

  ╔═════════════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  WHAT MAKES REWALSAR UNIQUE             ║
  ║                                         ║
  ║  Three faiths share one shoreline:      ║
  ║  ▸ Sikhs: Guru Gobind Singh camped      ║
  ║    here. Gurudwara on the eastern bank. ║
  ║  ▸ Hindus: Sage Lomas meditates here.  ║
  ║    Three temples on the hillside.       ║
  ║  ▸ Buddhists: Guru Padmasambhava        ║
  ║    (Rinpoche) attained enlightenment    ║
  ║    here. Three monasteries. This is     ║
  ║    "Tso Pema" — the Lotus Lake.        ║
  ║                                         ║
  ║  All three communities hold festivals   ║
  ║  at the same lake. Nobody argues.       ║
  ╚═════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 1  ·  CHANDIGARH → MANDI → REWALSAR
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Drive from Chandigarh to Mandi (190 km, 4.5 hrs).
  Stop in Mandi for lunch — this is a temple town that
  barely gets tourist attention despite having 81 stone
  shrines within its boundaries.

  Essential Mandi stops:
  ▸ Panchvaktra Temple (5-faced Shiva, riverbank)
  ▸ Triloknath Temple (16th century, Nagari style)
  ▸ Old Mandi market for local textiles and spices

  After lunch: drive 24 km to Rewalsar Lake (1,360m).
  The lake is small — you can walk the perimeter in
  40 minutes. But the density of sacred architecture
  around it is extraordinary: prayer wheels, chortens,
  floating islands, coloured monastery walls, a giant
  Padmasambhava statue watching over everything.

  Evening: walk the lakeside circuit. The floating
  islands (reed islands that drift slowly) are a
  mystery — they move with no discernible wind.
  Feed the massive sacred fish (carp) that surface
  for offerings at the ghats.

  TONIGHT  →  Guesthouse at Rewalsar  ₹700–1,500

  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 2  ·  MONASTERY CIRCUIT + HILLSIDE TEMPLES
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Morning: Visit all three monasteries before the
  tourist groups arrive (before 9 AM they are yours
  alone). The Zigar Drukpa Kargyud Monastery has
  the best murals. The Nyingma monastery has the
  most atmospheric butter lamp hall.

  10:30  Gurudwara Rewalsar Sahib: immaculate white
  marble complex. Langar served to all. Sit for the
  kirtan for 20 minutes at least — the acoustics in
  the marble hall are something else.

  Afternoon: Trek up to the hilltop temples above
  the lake — Lomas Rishi cave and two small shrines.
  The view down onto the lake from above reveals
  its perfect round shape, the three colours of the
  monastery roofs reflected in the water.

  TONIGHT  →  Rewalsar  ₹700–1,500

  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 3  ·  PRASHAR LAKE (DAY TRIP) → DEPART
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Drive 25 km from Rewalsar to Mandi, then 49 km
  on mountain road to Prashar Lake (2,730m). The
  floating island lake with the pagoda temple.

  Two sacred lakes in two days — both with floating
  islands, both unexplained by science.

  Return to Mandi, then Chandigarh (arrive ~8 PM).

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  QUICK REFERENCE                                 │
  │  Nearest railway:  Mandi (no direct train;       │
  │                    bus from Chandigarh)          │
  │  Best local guide: Ask at the Zigar monastery   │
  │  Festival timing:  Mandi Shivratri (Feb/Mar)    │
  │                    — largest hill festival in HP │
  │  Photography:      Sacred fish + floating island │
  │                    best at golden hour           │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  FOOD: Mandi Dham (traditional thali), Bhey (lotus
  stem curry), Aktori, Mittha (sweet rice pudding
  with dry fruits), Monastery butter tea.



┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               ✦ ADVENTURE SPECIAL ✦                     │
│          I T I N E R A R Y   N o .  1 8                 │
│                                                         │
│    BEAS KUND TREK — THE SOURCE OF THE SACRED RIVER     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  DURATION       4 Days  (2 trek + 2 travel)
  REGION         Solang Nullah → Beas Kund Glacier
  THEME          High-altitude glacial lake trek
  SEASON         June – October
  STARTS AT      Manali
  DIFFICULTY     ★★★☆☆  Moderate
  BUDGET         ₹10,000 – ₹18,000 / person
                 (incl. guide, camping)

  ╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  ALTITUDE PROFILE                                 ║
  ║                                                   ║
  ║  Solang (2,480m) ──▶ Dhundi (2,900m)             ║
  ║       ──▶ Bakarthach (3,350m)                    ║
  ║       ──▶ BEAS KUND (3,700m)  ← glacier lake    ║
  ║                                                   ║
  ║  Total distance:  14 km one way                   ║
  ║  Trek days:       2 up, 1 down                    ║
  ║  Max altitude:    3,700m (do not run)             ║
  ╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ═══════════════
  DAY 1  ·  MANALI PREP
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Arrive Manali. Spend the day acclimatising properly.
  Walk Old Manali. Hire gear at one of the trek shops
  on Mall Road (sleeping bag, trekking poles, rain
  cover — about ₹300–500/day rental).

  Evening briefing with your guide. Check weather.
  Beas Kund is in the shadow of the Pir Panjal and
  gets sudden storms — have a contingency plan.

  Hire guide through Manali-registered trek operators
  on Mall Road or Old Manali (₹1,200–1,500/day).

  TONIGHT  →  Hotel, Manali  ₹1,500–3,000

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 2  ·  MANALI → DHUNDI → BAKARTHACH CAMP
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Drive to Solang (13 km from Manali, 30 min).
  Trek from the Solang base toward Dhundi (6 km,
  2 hours) through meadows used by shepherds
  in summer — the Gaddi community with their
  enormous white flocks.

  Beyond Dhundi the valley narrows and the trail
  begins climbing in earnest through boulderfields
  and moraines. The Pir Panjal peaks close in on
  both sides. Friendship Peak (5,289m) is ahead.

  Camp at Bakarthach meadow (3,350m). The campsite
  sits in a wide bowl with the glacier clearly visible
  above. At night the temperature drops to 0–5°C.
  The stars are absolute.

  TONIGHT  →  Camp at Bakarthach
  DISTANCE  →  12 km total today, ~5–6 hours

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 3  ·  BAKARTHACH → BEAS KUND → RETURN
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Wake at 6 AM. The light on Friendship Peak at
  dawn is rose-gold and the mountain looks close
  enough to touch.

  Trek 2 km uphill to Beas Kund (3,700m) — the
  glacial lake that is the source of the Beas River.
  The same river that flows through Kullu, through
  Mandi, through Punjab, and eventually joins the
  Sutlej. It starts here, at this cold green lake
  beneath a glacier, in absolute silence.

  Spend an hour at the lake. Drink the water. Feel
  the cold. Understand why the river is sacred.

  Descend all the way back to Dhundi and drive to
  Manali. Arrive by evening.

  TONIGHT  →  Hotel, Manali  ₹1,500–3,000

  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════
  DAY 4  ·  REST + HADIMBA + DEPARTURE
  ═══════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Morning: your legs will request a rest. Oblige.
  Hadimba Devi Temple at 10 AM (crowd-free on
  weekdays). Vashisht hot springs to finish.

  Evening: overnight Volvo to Delhi or drive to
  Bhuntar for morning flight.

  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │  GEAR CHECKLIST                                  │
  │  ✓ Trekking boots (not sneakers — the boulders  │
  │    above Dhundi require ankle support)           │
  │  ✓ Trekking poles — mandatory on the moraine    │
  │  ✓ Sunscreen SPF 50+ (glacier UV is extreme)    │
  │  ✓ Waterproof jacket (afternoon storms common)  │
  │  ✓ Thermal base layer for camp nights           │
  │  ✓ Water: 2 litres minimum; refill at streams   │
  │  ✓ Altitude sickness pills (Diamox) — consult   │
  │    a doctor before the trek                     │
  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  FOOD ON TREK: Your cook/guide provides. Expect
  dal-chawal, vegetable curry, parathas, instant
  soup at camp. Carry: chocolate, nuts, electrolyte
  powder, energy bars.

│                                                         │
│  SHIMLA TO NARKANDA — THE APPLE TRAIL FOR FAMILIES     │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  DURATION       8 Days
  REGION         Shimla, Kufri, Chail, Narkanda,
                 Hatu Peak, Sarahan, Rampur
  THEME          Family-friendly hill stations +
                 apple orchards + easy nature walks
  SEASON         Sep–Oct (apple harvest) OR
                 Dec–Jan (snow) OR Mar–May (flowers)
  STARTS AT      Shimla (Chandigarh → Shimla: 120 km)
  DIFFICULTY     ★★☆☆☆  Easy (family suitable)
  BUDGET         ₹18,000 – ₹30,000 / family of 4

  ╔═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  WHY THIS WORKS FOR FAMILIES                      ║
  ║                                                   ║
  ║  ▸ All driving — no trekking required            ║
  ║  ▸ Hotel options at every stop                   ║
  ║  ▸ Apple picking season has kid-specific farms   ║
  ║  ▸ No altitude above 3,000m — no AMS risk       ║
  ║  ▸ Lots of "wow" moments without stress          ║
  ╚═══════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAY 1–2  ·  SHIMLA
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Kids love:
  ▸ The Toy Train from Kalka (4-hour journey — book
    window seats weeks ahead; the 103 tunnels and
    800 bridges are a moving picture show)
  ▸ Jakhu Hill monkeys — chaotic, funny, memorable
    (keep snacks hidden)
  ▸ Ice skating rink at Lakkar Bazaar (Dec–Feb)
  ▸ Himachal State Museum with its miniature
    paintings and dioramas of hill life

  Adults love:
  ▸ The Scandal Point story (Google it before going)
  ▸ Gorton Castle architecture
  ▸ Glen Forest walk (3 km, flat, gorgeous)

  TONIGHT  →  Heritage hotel near The Ridge
              ₹3,500–7,000/night

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAY 3  ·  KUFRI + FAGU
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Kufri (2,600m, 16 km from Shimla): the Himalayan
  Nature Park has Himalayan black bears, snow leopards
  (captive), Himalayan monal pheasant (in the wild),
  and yaks for kids to ride. It is touristy and
  the family will love every second of it.

  Fagu (2,450m): 8 km further, a quieter apple
  village. Walk through the orchards in September —
  the trees hang heavy with Himachali apples in
  every size and variety. Farmers let you pick for
  a small fee.

  TONIGHT  →  Resort in Kufri or Fagu  ₹3,000–6,000

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAY 4  ·  CHAIL
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Drive 45 km to Chail (2,250m). The Maharaja's
  revenge summer capital. The world's highest cricket
  ground at 2,444m — take a family photo on the pitch.
  Kids can bowl on it (no one stops you off-season).

  Chail Wildlife Sanctuary: deer, peafowl, and the
  occasional Himalayan barking deer visible from
  the forest roads even without trekking.

  TONIGHT  →  Chail Palace Hotel (splurge) or
              forest guesthouse  ₹2,500–8,000

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAY 5  ·  NARKANDA + HATU PEAK
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Drive back toward Shimla and then east on NH5 to
  Narkanda (2,708m, 65 km). This sleepy apple town
  has one extraordinary trick: a 3 km trail to Hatu
  Peak (3,300m) through apple orchards so dense in
  September that the ground is carpeted with fallen
  fruit and the air smells like a cider press.

  Hatu Peak summit: a small Hatu Mata temple and
  the widest view in the lower Shimla hills. On
  clear days: the Kinnaur peaks to the east, the
  Shimla ranges behind, and the Sutlej valley below.

  TONIGHT  →  HPTDC Hotel Hatu, Narkanda  ₹2,500–4,000

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAYS 6–7  ·  SARAHAN + RAMPUR
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Continue east 80 km to Sarahan (2,165m).
  Bhimakali Temple complex: a hybrid of Himalayan-
  Pahari and North Indian temple architecture.
  The inner sanctum allows non-Hindus with a
  (provided) dhoti and head covering.

  Day 7: Rampur Bushahr — the old capital of a
  Himalayan kingdom. The Padam Palace, Lavi Fair
  grounds, and a riverside market that sold goods
  from China, Tibet, Ladakh, and the plains
  simultaneously for centuries.

  TONIGHT  →  Guesthouse in Rampur  ₹1,000–2,500

  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄
  DAY 8  ·  TATTAPANI HOT SPRINGS → SHIMLA → HOME
  ┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄┄

  Return journey via Tattapani (45 km from Rampur)
  for a morning soak in the Sutlej-bank hot springs.
  Kids + hot springs + cold river beside = guaranteed
  memory. Then drive back to Shimla (85 km) and
  onward to Chandigarh.

  FOOD FOR FAMILIES: Himachali rajma-chawal (the
  kids will eat it), fresh apple juice straight
  from orchards, Pahari aloo (mountain potatoes
  with herbs), Bhatura from Shimla's Lower Bazaar,
  Apple jam on toast at any Narkanda guesthouse.



┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              ✦ ULTIMATE ROAD TRIP ✦                     │
│         I T I N E R A R Y   N o .  2 0                  │
│                                                         │
│  THE ENTIRE HIMACHAL LOOP — DELHI BACK TO DELHI        │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  DURATION       10 Days
  REGION         All of Himachal — one continuous loop
  THEME          Grand road trip: plains to desert
                 to forest to pilgrimage to plains
  SEASON         June – September (best window for
                 both Spiti and coastal Himachal)
  STARTS AT      Delhi (self-drive or hired cab)
  DIFFICULTY     ★★★☆☆  Moderate (long drives)
  BUDGET         ₹25,000 – ₹45,000 / person
                 (depending heavily on vehicle + stays)

  ╔═════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
  ║  THE FULL ROUTE AT A GLANCE                         ║
  ║                                                     ║
  ║  Delhi → Chandigarh → Shimla → Narkanda →          ║
  ║  Rampur → Kalpa (Kinnaur) → Nako → Tabo →          ║
  ║  Kaza (Spiti) → Chandratal → Manali →               ║
  ║  Dharamshala → Chandigarh → Delhi                   ║
  ║                                                     ║
  ║  Total distance:  ~2,200 km                         ║
  ║  Driving days:    10 (averaging 180–250 km/day)    ║
  ║  Mountain driving: 7 out of 10 days                 ║
  ╚═════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 1  ·  DELHI → SHIMLA  (350 km · 8 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Leave Delhi by 5 AM. Highway to Chandigarh (4 hrs).
  Stop for a proper Punjabi lunch at Murthal on the
  way (₹200 for a full thali — legendary).

  Join the old Hindustan-Tibet Road at Kalka. Enter
  the Shivalik hills. The first ridgeline crossing
  at Kasauli brings the first Himalayan views.

  Arrive Shimla by afternoon. Walk the Mall Road
  for an hour. Sleep deeply.

  TONIGHT  →  Shimla heritage hotel  ₹2,500–5,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 2  ·  SHIMLA → RAMPUR  (165 km · 5 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Down the Sutlej valley all day. Stop at Narkanda
  for breakfast and a 30-minute Hatu Peak viewpoint
  walk (don't do the full trek — save legs for later).
  Reach Rampur by lunch. See the Padam Palace and
  the old Lavi Fair grounds. Drive on.

  TONIGHT  →  Guesthouse, Rampur  ₹900–2,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 3  ·  RAMPUR → KALPA  (90 km · 3.5 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Into Kinnaur. The cliff road begins at Wangtu —
  a single-lane ledge above the Sutlej gorge with
  a 500m vertical drop below the passenger window.
  (Sit on the valley side, not the cliff side.)

  Stop at Spillo for photos — the gorge is at its
  most dramatic here. Reach Reckong Peo for lunch.

  Drive 11 km up to Kalpa (2,960m). Check in.
  Watch the sunset on Kinner Kailash from your
  room window or from the apple orchards.

  TONIGHT  →  Kalpa guesthouse  ₹1,500–3,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 4  ·  KALPA → NAKO → TABO  (175 km · 6 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  The road leaves Kinnaur's green valleys and enters
  the rain-shadow desert of Upper Kinnaur. The
  landscape becomes lunar within 20 km.

  Nako village and its 10th-century monastery by
  the glacial lake — 1 hour stop minimum.

  Enter Spiti at Khab (confluence of Spiti and Sutlej).
  The difference is total and immediate: brown, wide,
  silent, Tibetan.

  Tabo (3,050m): the "Ajanta of the Himalayas."
  The monastery's clay sculptures and 1,000-year-old
  murals are the finest in any Himalayan monastery.
  Arrive in time for the evening prayers.

  TONIGHT  →  Monastery guesthouse, Tabo  ₹800–1,500

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 5  ·  TABO → DHANKAR → PIN → KAZA  (85 km)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Morning: Dhankar Monastery (35 km from Tabo).
  The monastery on the cliff above the river
  confluence is the most dramatic monastic site in
  all of Spiti. Vertical drop below the monastery
  walls. Walk up carefully.

  Afternoon: detour into Pin Valley — 25 km to Mudh
  and back. The emptiest valley in India.

  Evening: Kaza. Hot meal. Real bed.

  TONIGHT  →  Hotel in Kaza  ₹1,200–2,500

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 6  ·  KAZA → KEY → KIBBER → CHANDRATAL
                                        (110 km)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Morning: Key Monastery at dawn (4,166m) — monks
  doing puja, the valley below in shadow, the peaks
  lit. One of the great dawn moments in India.

  Kibber village and Chicham Bridge. Fossils at
  Langza. Hikkim post office postcard (send one home).

  Drive to Batal and then 14 km off-road to
  Chandratal Lake (4,300m). Camp.

  TONIGHT  →  Tent camp at Chandratal  ₹1,500–2,500

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 7  ·  CHANDRATAL → MANALI  (115 km · 5 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Dawn at Chandratal. The lake at 5 AM with no one
  else there is one of the high points of any life.

  Drive over Kunzum Pass (4,590m), through Lahaul's
  wide brown valley, through the Atal Tunnel, and
  down into the green Kullu valley. The colour change
  at the Rohtang/tunnel north exit is shocking after
  a week in the desert.

  Arrive Manali by afternoon. Hot shower.
  Long dinner. Sleep hard.

  TONIGHT  →  Old Manali hotel  ₹1,500–4,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 8  ·  MANALI REST + VASHISHT + NAGGAR
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Your body needs this day. Morning in Vashisht hot
  springs. Hadimba Devi Temple. Late lunch in Old
  Manali at a rooftop café with a Beas valley view.

  Afternoon: drive 37 km to Naggar. Roerich Gallery.
  Castle terrace dinner if budget allows.

  TONIGHT  →  Naggar or Manali  ₹1,200–4,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 9  ·  MANALI → DHARAMSHALA  (240 km · 7 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Leave Manali through the Kullu Valley. Stop at
  Mandi for lunch (Panchvaktra temple, riverside).

  Continue to Dharamshala (McLeod Ganj). Check in.
  Evening walk at the Tsuglagkhang complex — the
  Tibetan flag, the prayer wheels, the monks.

  TONIGHT  →  McLeod Ganj guesthouse  ₹1,000–3,000

  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  DAY 10  ·  DHARAMSHALA → CHANDIGARH → DELHI
                                    (370 km · 9 hrs)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Morning: Masroor Rock-Cut Temples detour (1 hr off
  route — do not skip) or Kangra Fort depending on
  which way you exit Dharamshala.

  Drive to Chandigarh (180 km, 4 hrs). Coffee at
  Sector 17. Walk the Rock Garden if there is time.

  Chandigarh to Delhi (250 km, 4.5 hrs). Arrive
  home with 2,200 km of Himachal in your body.

  FOOD ACROSS THE LOOP: Murthal parathas (Day 1),
  Narkanda apple (Day 2), Kinnauri rajma (Day 3),

  hummus (Day 8), Mandi Dham (Day 9), Kangra tea
  to finish (Day 10).
