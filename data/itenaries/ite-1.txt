Head back to Chandigarh or Delhi for your return journey [17, 21].2. The "Serene & Spiritual" (Dharamshala & Dalhousie)Best for: Culture seekers and peace lovers.Day 1–2: Dharamshala & McLeodganj. Visit the Tsuglagkhang Complex (Dalai Lama Temple), Bhagsu Falls, and take a short trek to Dharamkot [3, 6].Day 3: Bir Billing. A day trip to the Paragliding Capital of India to experience tandem flights or explore the monasteries [3, 25].Day 4: Dharamshala to Dalhousie. Drive to the colonial hill station of Dalhousie and explore the local churches [5, 21].Day 5: Khajjiar. Day trip to the Mini Switzerland of India, famous for its vast meadows and cedar forests [9, 21].Day 6: Chamba or Dainkund Peak. Visit the historic temples of Chamba or hike to Dainkund for 360-degree valley views [19, 21].Day 7: Depart. Travel toward Pathankot or Amritsar for departure [15, 21].3. The "Offbeat Adventure" (Spiti Valley Circuit)Best for: Backpackers and road-trippers (Note: Requires 9+ days for a full circuit).Day 1–2: Shimla to Sangla/Chitkul. Drive into the Kinnaur Valley to visit the last village on the Indo-Tibetan border [21, 23].Day 3: Kalpa.
