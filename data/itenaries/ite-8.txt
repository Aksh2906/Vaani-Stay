┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FIELD REPORT № 31                               [ KINNAUR ] │
│                                                               │
│  KINNER KAILASH PARIKRAMA —                                  │
│  CIRCUMAMBULATING SHIVA'S MOUNTAIN                           │
│                                                               │
│  Dateline: Charang village, 3,150m, Kinnaur district         │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  VITALS AT A GLANCE
  Duration 8 days  ·  Max altitude 5,300m  ·  Difficulty ★★★★★
  Season July–September  ·  Start Charang, End Chitkul
  Budget ₹22,000–38,000/person  ·  Permit mandatory

  ═══ REPORTER'S NOTEBOOK ════════════════════════════════════

  The Kinner Kailash is not one peak but a range — a wall
  of granite and ice 35 km long above the Sutlej valley in
  Kinnaur. Popularly known as the mountain of Lord Shiva,
  it rises to 6,500 metres and draws pilgrims who come to
  perform the holy parikrama — a clockwise circumambulation
  of the sacred peak — starting from Morang and ending
  in Kalpa. 

  The trekking circuit, however, takes a higher and harder
  line. The Charang-La pass sits at 5,300m and the
  holy Kinner Kailash Shivling itself stands at approximately
  4,800m. Clockwise circumambulation — Parikrama — is done
  around the holy peak.  The trek starts from Charang
  village and ends at Chitkul in the Baspa Valley. Charang-La
  forms a drainage divide between Tidong valley and Baspa
  valley of Kinnaur. 

  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❝ The Shivling at Kinner Kailash changes colour
    through the day — white at dawn, gold at noon,
    rose at sunset, silver at night. Pilgrims watch
    it for hours and call each shade a different
    blessing. ❞
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ─── THE ROUTE, DAY BY DAY ──────────────────────────────

  Day 1  │ DELHI/SHIMLA → RECKONG PEO → KALPA
         │ Arrive Kalpa (2,960m). This is your last
         │ comfortable night. The view of Kinner Kailash
         │ from Kalpa — the entire range lit at sunset
         │ across the valley — is the motivation you
         │ carry for the next seven days.
         │ STAY: Guesthouse in Kalpa  ₹1,500–3,000

  Day 2  │ KALPA → MORANG → CHARANG BASE
         │ Drive to Morang (15 km from Peo) — the
         │ official parikrama start point. Registration
         │ with district administration here (mandatory).
         │ Trek 8 km to Charang village (3,150m) through
         │ terraced apple orchards and juniper scrub.
         │ Charang is a Kinnauri village of perhaps
         │ 30 families — warm, utterly genuine.
         │ STAY: Homestay in Charang  ₹800–1,200

  Day 3  │ CHARANG → LALANTI CAMP  (3,700m · 10 km)
         │ The climb begins in earnest. Oak forest
         │ gives way to alpine scrub and then open
         │ moraine. The Kinner Kailash Shivling — the
         │ 4,800m natural rock formation — is visible
         │ on the right all day, growing impossibly large.
         │ Camp at Lalanti meadow above the treeline.
         │ CAMP: Lalanti  ₹ guide-arranged

  Day 4  │ LALANTI → CHARANG-LA BASE CAMP  (4,400m)
         │ Short but altitude-heavy day. Rest here.
         │ Look north: the Tidong valley drops away,
         │ the Tibet ranges are visible beyond.
         │ The pass is visible above — a notch in
         │ an enormous ridge of dark rock and snow.
         │ CAMP: Below Charang-La  (4,400m)

  Day 5  │ CHARANG-LA CROSSING  (5,300m) ★ SUMMIT DAY
         │ Leave 4 AM. The climb to 5,300m is the
         │ highest point in this report series —
         │ higher than anything in the Spiti itinerary,
         │ higher than Hamta, higher than Rupin.
         │ At the pass: both worlds visible. The Tidong
         │ valley behind. The Baspa valley — Sangla,
         │ Chitkul — far below ahead. Descent is steep
         │ scree/snow. Take your time.
         │ Camp on Baspa side at 4,000m.
         │ CAMP: Baspa side  (4,000m)

  Day 6  │ DESCENT TOWARD CHITKUL  (12 km)
         │ Long descent through increasingly green
         │ valley. The Baspa river appears. Poplars.
         │ Stone walls. Apple trees. Warmth returns.
         │ Camp at lower meadows near Chitkul.
         │ CAMP: Lower meadows  (3,200m)

  Day 7  │ ARRIVE CHITKUL
         │ The last village. You have walked around
         │ a sacred mountain range. Sit at the Mathi
         │ Devi temple at the valley end and feel
         │ what that means.
         │ STAY: Homestay, Chitkul  ₹1,000–1,800

  Day 8  │ CHITKUL → SHIMLA/DELHI (drive out)

  ─── PERMITS & PRACTICALITIES ───────────────────────────
  → District registration: Reckong Peo DC office
    (mandatory for Indians and foreigners)
  → Forest permit: ₹100/day from Peo forest office
  → Registered guide mandatory for Charang-La section
  → As per HP Miscellaneous Adventure Activities Rules
    2017, any trekking in Kinnaur requires prior
    permission from the tourism office and camping
    permission from the forest department. 
  → Best agency contact: Reckong Peo or Sangla-based
    registered operators

  ON THE PLATE: Kinnauri rajma · Chitkul homestay daal
  · Altitude chocolate at Charang-La · Baspa-side walnut
  chutney · Sangla trout on the final evening.




┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FIELD REPORT № 32                      [ KULLU / MANALI ]  │
│                                                               │
│  FRIENDSHIP PEAK EXPEDITION —                                │
│  MANALI'S ENTRY POINT TO MOUNTAINEERING                     │
│                                                               │
│  Dateline: Solang Valley, 2,480m, above Manali               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  VITALS AT A GLANCE
  Duration 9 days  ·  Summit altitude 5,289m  ·  ★★★★★
  Season May–June, September–October  ·  Start/End Manali
  Budget ₹25,000–42,000/person  ·  Technical gear required

  ═══ REPORTER'S NOTEBOOK ════════════════════════════════════

  Friendship Peak (5,289m) is what happens when a serious
  trekker decides to become a mountaineer. It sits directly
  above the Beas Kund glacier, in full view from the Solang
  Valley ski slopes where weekend tourists take selfies —
  but the mountain itself is a genuine technical climb.
  The trek starts from Solang Valley and takes you through
  beautiful forests of pine and oak, alpine meadows, and
  then into steep, snowy and rocky sections. To reach the
  summit you must cross glaciers and use technical equipment
  like ropes, harnesses, and ice axes. This expedition
  requires good fitness and prior experience in high-altitude
  trekking or mountaineering. 

  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❝ From Friendship Peak summit, the peaks line up
    like a sentence you have been trying to read
    your whole life: Hanuman Tibba, Shitidhar,
    Deo Tibba, Seven Sisters — all suddenly clear. ❞
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ─── THE ROUTE ──────────────────────────────────────────

  Day 1  │ MANALI: GEAR CHECK + BRIEFING
         │ Hire guide and porter through a Manali
         │ agency with IMF (Indian Mountaineering
         │ Federation) registered guides. Rent gear:
         │ crampons, ice axe, harness, helmet, rope.
         │ Medical check. Altitude orientation.
         │ STAY: Manali hotel  ₹1,500–3,000

  Day 2  │ MANALI → SOLANG → DHUNDI BASE  (2,900m)
         │ Drive to Solang (13 km). Trek 6 km through
         │ pine forest to Dhundi. The Pir Panjal wall
         │ is ahead. Friendship Peak is directly above,
         │ its summit snowfield visible from here.
         │ CAMP: Dhundi  ₹ agency-arranged

  Day 3  │ DHUNDI → BEAS KUND GLACIER  (3,700m)
         │ Trek 8 km to the Beas Kund glacier lake —
         │ source of the Beas River. Camp on the
         │ moraine above the lake. Glacier orientation
         │ session with guide: crampon technique,
         │ self-arrest with ice axe, rope procedures.
         │ CAMP: Above Beas Kund  (3,700m)

  Day 4  │ GLACIER TRAINING + ACCLIMATISATION
         │ Full day on the glacier practising:
         │ → Crampon walking on slope ice
         │ → Ice axe braking technique
         │ → Rope team movement
         │ → Fixed rope ascending (jumar technique)
         │ Rest afternoon. Early sleep.
         │ CAMP: Same  (3,700m)

  Day 5  │ ADVANCE BASE CAMP  (4,400m)
         │ Move up to ABC below the Friendship Peak
         │ face. 5 km, 4 hours on glacier and moraine.
         │ The peak is enormous from here — the
         │ summit snowfield 900m above your tent.
         │ CAMP: ABC  (4,400m)

  Day 6  │ SUMMIT ATTEMPT
         │ Leave 2 AM by headtorch. The climbing
         │ sections above ABC: mixed rock and ice,
         │ fixed rope on steep sections, final
         │ summit ridge in crampons.
         │ Once you reach the summit you are rewarded
         │ with 360-degree views of mountains like
         │ Hanuman Tibba, Shitidhar, and Deo Tibba. 
         │ Return to ABC by afternoon if successful.
         │ Descend to Beas Kund camp.
         │ CAMP: Beas Kund  (3,700m)

  Day 7  │ WEATHER/REST BUFFER DAY
         │ All expeditions need this. Use it if
         │ Day 6 was weather-blocked. Otherwise
         │ begin descent.

  Days 8–9  │ DESCENT TO SOLANG + MANALI DEPARTURE
            │ Day 8: Trek back to Dhundi and Solang.
            │ Manali hotel. Hot shower. Celebrate.
            │ Day 9: Bus/flight to Delhi.

  ─── EQUIPMENT LIST ─────────────────────────────────────
  TECHNICAL (hire from Manali agencies):
  ✦ 12-point crampons  ✦ Ice axe  ✦ Harness + belay
  ✦ Helmet  ✦ Jumar + descender  ✦ Fixed rope sections

  PERSONAL (bring your own):
  ✦ -20°C sleeping bag  ✦ 4-season tent (agency provides)
  ✦ Mountaineering boots (NOT trekking boots — different)
  ✦ Balaclava + goggles + 3-layer hand system
  ✦ High SPF glacier cream (sun burns at 5,000m)

  NOTE: First-timers on technical terrain MUST have
  at least 5+ high-altitude (4,000m+) treks before
  attempting Friendship Peak. This is a mountain, not
  an advanced trek.

  ON THE PLATE: Pre-expedition Manali dinner (eat well)
  · Camp cook meals for 6 days (agency-provided) ·
  Summit chocolate and hot soup at 5,289m · Manali
  apple cider brandy on return evening.




┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FIELD REPORT № 33                          [ KULLU VALLEY ] │
│                                                               │
│  DEO TIBBA BASE CAMP — THE PEAK THAT HOLDS UP THE SKY       │
│                                                               │
│  Dateline: Jagatsukh village, 1,400m, below Manali           │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  VITALS AT A GLANCE
  Duration 7 days  ·  Max altitude 4,600m  ·  ★★★★☆
  Season May–October  ·  Start/End Jagatsukh (near Manali)
  Budget ₹16,000–26,000/person  ·  Guide strongly advised

  ═══ REPORTER'S NOTEBOOK ════════════════════════════════════

  Deo Tibba (6,001m) dominates the eastern skyline above
  Manali — a massive pyramid of rock and permanent ice that
  watches over the entire Kullu Valley. Most people look at
  it from Manali Mall Road without knowing its name.
  The Base Camp trek approaches it from Jagatsukh village
  through a series of valley systems that lead ever deeper
  into the mountain's shadow, past glacial lakes with no
  names on any map, through meadows where no tourist
  infrastructure exists.

  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❝ Deo Tibba translates roughly as "God's Plateau."
    From its base camp at 4,600 metres, you understand
    the name immediately — the mountain fills the
    sky so completely that nothing else exists. ❞
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ─── THE ROUTE ──────────────────────────────────────────

  Day 1  │ MANALI → JAGATSUKH → KHANOL  (2,450m)
         │ Drive 6 km south of Manali to Jagatsukh —
         │ an ancient Kullu village with a Shiva temple
         │ of remarkable carved stonework. This was
         │ the capital of Kullu before Naggar.
         │ Begin trek. Trail climbs through cultivated
         │ terraces and then forest. Camp at Khanol
         │ meadow (2,450m) after 8 km (4 hours).
         │ CAMP: Khanol  ₹ guide-arranged

  Day 2  │ KHANOL → CHHIKA CAMP  (3,380m · 10 km)
         │ Dense deodar and birch forest. The valley
         │ narrows as you climb. First views of the
         │ high peaks appear above the treeline —
         │ Deo Tibba's glacier is suddenly visible.
         │ Chhika: wide open meadow, stream running
         │ through it, first truly alpine camp.
         │ CAMP: Chhika  (3,380m)

  Day 3  │ CHHIKA → CHOTA CHANDRATAL LAKE  (4,200m)
         │ Above the treeline completely. The trail
         │ climbs through boulder fields to the Chota
         │ Chandratal (little moon lake) — a glacial
         │ tarn with Deo Tibba reflected in its surface
         │ on calm mornings. The silence here is the
         │ kind that has weight.
         │ CAMP: Chota Chandratal  (4,200m)

  Day 4  │ DEO TIBBA BASE CAMP  (4,600m)
         │ Two-hour climb from the lake to the true
         │ base camp below the Deo Tibba glacier face.
         │ The summit pyramid from here is enormous —
         │ 1,400 vertical metres of ice and rock.
         │ Afternoon: exploratory walk on the glacier
         │ snout with guide (crampons needed).
         │ Watch the ice calve in the afternoon sun.
         │ CAMP: Base Camp  (4,600m)

  Day 5  │ BASE CAMP → CHHIKA  (descent)
         │ Retrace to Chhika. The descent always
         │ reveals different details — plants you
         │ didn't notice climbing, a stream crossing
         │ that was harder going up, light at a new angle.
         │ CAMP: Chhika  (3,380m)

  Days 6–7  │ CHHIKA → JAGATSUKH + MANALI + DEPARTURE
            │ Day 6: Full descent to Jagatsukh.
            │ Manali hotel. Legs rest. Stomach celebrates.
            │ Day 7: Bus or flight to Delhi.

  ─── FIELD NOTES ────────────────────────────────────────
  → The Jagatsukh starting point is 6 km from Manali —
    most agencies will arrange a jeep pickup at 6 AM
  → Chota Chandratal is the photographic highlight:
    bring your best lens and set an alarm for pre-dawn
  → The glacier above base camp is crevassed — do NOT
    walk it without a guide and crampon experience
  → This trek is significantly less crowded than Hamta
    Pass or Beas Kund, despite being more beautiful
  → A side trip to the Chandrakhani Pass (add 2 days
    from Naggar, 15 km away) pairs perfectly with this

  ON THE PLATE: Jagatsukh temple prasad (jaggery +
  sesame) · Khanol camp cook daal-roti · Wild herbs
  collected by cook at Chhika · Manali post-trek thali
  (eat two of them) · Apple crumble at Old Manali café.




┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  FIELD REPORT № 34                   [ KANGRA / DHAULADHAR ] │
│                                                               │
│  INDRAHAR PASS — THE DHAULADHAR CROSSING TO CHAMBA           │
│                                                               │
│  Dateline: McLeod Ganj, 1,457m, Dharamshala                  │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  VITALS AT A GLANCE
  Duration 6 days  ·  Max altitude 4,342m  ·  ★★★★☆
  Season May–June, September–October  ·  Start McLeod
  End Brahmaur, Chamba  ·  Budget ₹13,000–22,000/person

  ═══ REPORTER'S NOTEBOOK ════════════════════════════════════

  Most Dharamshala visitors look north at the Dhauladhar
  wall and feel its scale from a safe distance. A handful
  go to Triund and feel it closer. Very few cross it.

  The Indrahar Pass (4,342m) crosses the Dhauladhar and
  descends into Chamba on the other side — from the Tibetan
  cultural world of McLeod Ganj into the Hindu temple world
  of Brahmaur, the ancient Chamba capital. It is the kind
  of pass where the mountain truly separates two worlds, and
  crossing it on foot makes you feel the separation and
  the connection simultaneously.

  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❝ You stand at Indrahar Pass and look back at
    the Kangra Valley spread out thirty kilometres
    below — the entire valley you have been looking
    up at the mountains from. Then you turn north
    and the Chamba ranges begin. ❞
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ─── THE ROUTE ──────────────────────────────────────────

  Day 1  │ McLEOD GANJ: PREP + BHAGSU TEMPLE
         │ Arrive McLeod. Hire guide through any of
         │ the established trekking agencies on
         │ Jogibara Road (₹1,200–1,500/day). Gear
         │ check. Evening walk to Bhagsu Nag Temple
         │ and waterfall — you will pass this again
         │ on Day 2 at dawn.
         │ STAY: McLeod Ganj guesthouse  ₹800–2,500

  Day 2  │ McLEOD → TRIUND RIDGE  (2,828m · 9 km)
         │ Early start via Gallu Devi Temple.
         │ This is the well-trodden Triund trail —
         │ but this time you do not stop at the ridge.
         │ Pass Triund camp, wave at the day-trippers,
         │ and continue northeast up the Laka glacier
         │ moraine toward higher ground.
         │ Laka Got camp at 3,350m is the overnight.
         │ The Dhauladhar wall is now all around you.
         │ CAMP: Laka Got  (3,350m)

  Day 3  │ LAKA GOT → INDRAHAR PASS → LAHESH CAVE
         │ (4,342m · hardest day · 12 km total)
         │
         │ The climb to Indrahar from Laka Got:
         │ steep, sometimes snow, always dramatic.
         │ The final 200m is a rocky scramble to the
         │ pass notch at 4,342m.
         │
         │ At the top: Kangra Valley visible behind
         │ you 2,900m below. The Chamba ranges open
         │ ahead in waves of brown and grey. The Ravi
         │ watershed begins.
         │
         │ Descent to Lahesh Cave on the Chamba side —
         │ a natural cave used as a shelter for centuries
         │ by pilgrims, shepherds, and now trekkers.
         │ Your first night in Chamba district.
         │ CAMP: Lahesh Cave  (3,700m)

  Day 4  │ LAHESH CAVE → CHOBIA PASS → KUARSI  (3,950m)
         │ The Chamba side opens into wide meadows.
         │ Optional side-pass: Chobia Pass (4,319m)
         │ adds 3 km but gives views of the Manimahesh
         │ Kailash range — sacred peak of Chamba.
         │ Camp at Kuarsi meadow.
         │ CAMP: Kuarsi  (3,450m)

  Day 5  │ KUARSI → BRAHMAUR  (descent · 12 km)
         │ Long descent into the Ravi watershed.
         │ Forest returns — conifer, then oak.
         │ Villages appear. Women in Gaddi dress.
         │ Reach Brahmaur — ancient capital of Chamba
         │ kingdom (7th–10th century). The Chaurasi
         │ Temple complex here: 84 stone and wooden
         │ temples in a single courtyard, the oldest
         │ dating to the 7th century.
         │ STAY: Brahmaur guesthouse  ₹800–1,500

  Day 6  │ BRAHMAUR → CHAMBA → PATHANKOT
         │ Morning: Brahmaur temples at leisure —
         │ the Manimahesh Temple, the Laxminarayan
         │ temple complex. Truly ancient stone.
         │ Drive to Chamba town (65 km, 2 hours).
         │ Bhuri Singh Museum. Lakshmi Narayan temples.
         │ Evening: drive to Pathankot (90 km) for
         │ train or onward travel.

  ─── CROSSING LOGISTICS ─────────────────────────────────
  → The pass can be snow-covered May–June: gaiters +
    micro-spikes minimum, crampons in heavy snow years
  → Weather changes fast on the Dhauladhar: start
    all summit days before 7 AM
  → Brahmaur is accessible by road from Chamba:
    arrange a pickup from the valley if needed
  → The Indrahar Pass trail is marked but not always
    obvious on the upper sections — guide is advised

  ON THE PLATE: McLeod Tibetan café breakfast ·
  Triund trail Maggi (mandatory ritual) · Shepherd
  chai at Laka Got · Lahesh Cave instant soup ·
  Brahmaur village rajma · Chamba market chukh paste.

│  FIELD REPORT № 35                        [ KULLU DISTRICT ] │
│                                                               │
│  CHANDRAKHANI PASS + MAGIC VALLEY —                         │
│  THE RIDGE WALK ABOVE MALANA                                 │
│                                                               │
│  Dateline: Naggar village, 1,851m, Kullu Valley              │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  VITALS AT A GLANCE
  Duration 5 days  ·  Max altitude 3,660m  ·  ★★★☆☆
  Season May–October  ·  Start/End Naggar
  Budget ₹11,000–18,000/person  ·  Guide recommended

  ═══ REPORTER'S NOTEBOOK ════════════════════════════════════

  Chandrakhani Pass (3,660m) sits directly above Naggar
  and the Roerich Estate — the same ridge that painter
  Nicholas Roerich stared at every day from 1929 to 1947
  while producing his greatest Himalayan canvases.
  The pass is also the ridge above Malana village on
  the other side — so this trek gives you the full
  Naggar-Chandrakhani-Malana circuit in 5 days.

  The ridge trek from Chandrakhani Pass to Magic Valley
  is unique for its ridge walk. The summit features snow
  patches and numerous peaks including Hanuman Tibba and
  Friendship Peak. Kullu Valley's panoramic vistas and
  snow-capped summits enhance the beauty of the area. 

  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  ❝ The medieval legend says the gods descended to
    earth and became these peaks: Indrasan, Pir Panjal,
    Deo Tibba, Parvati. Standing at Chandrakhani
    with all four visible, the story stops being
    a legend entirely. ❞  — Chandrakhani Pass folklore 
  ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  ─── THE ROUTE ──────────────────────────────────────────

  Day 1  │ NAGGAR: ARRIVE + ROERICH GALLERY + CASTLE
         │ Drive from Manali or Kullu (20–37 km).
         │ Naggar Castle (16th-century kath-kuni
         │ construction) and the Nicholas Roerich
         │ Art Gallery — his Himalayan paintings
         │ line entire walls. Look at them, then
         │ look at the mountains outside. Repeat.
         │ Evening: walk the old Naggar village lanes.
         │ STAY: Castle hotel or guesthouse  ₹1,200–6,000

  Day 2  │ NAGGAR → RUMSU → NAYA TAPRU CAMP  (3,200m)
         │ Drive or walk 6 km to Rumsu village —
         │ traditional Kullu architecture, carved
         │ wooden houses, ancient temple.
         │ Trek begins through blue pine, deodar,
         │ walnut, golden oak, and wild cherry forest
         │ that provides a broad perspective of the
         │ Kullu region and its cloud-touching peaks. 
         │ Camp at Naya Tapru meadow (3,200m).
         │ CAMP: Naya Tapru  ₹ guide-arranged

  Day 3  │ NAYA TAPRU → CHANDRAKHANI PASS → MAGIC VALLEY
         │ Morning: summit push to Chandrakhani Pass
         │ (3,660m). The last 2 km is open ridge —
         │ exposed, windy, sensational.
         │
         │ AT THE PASS: Kullu Valley behind, the
         │ Malana Nala dropping steeply ahead,
         │ Hanuman Tibba and Friendship Peak on the
         │ skyline,  Deo Tibba to the east. This is
         │ one of the great viewpoints of the Kullu.
         │
         │ Descend toward "Magic Valley" — the
         │ local name for the meadow system between
         │ Chandrakhani and the Malana watershed.
         │ Wide, flowered, empty, magical.
         │ CAMP: Magic Valley  (3,400m)

  Day 4  │ MAGIC VALLEY → MALANA VILLAGE (VIEW) → JARI
         │ Trek down the Malana Nala past the upper
         │ Malana village area. Rules apply as in
         │ Itinerary № 25 — do not touch walls,
         │ respect all boundaries.
         │ Continue descent to Jari on the main road
         │ (12 km, 4 hours).
         │ Taxi to Kasol or Bhuntar.
         │ STAY: Kasol guesthouse  ₹600–1,200

  Day 5  │ KASOL → BHUNTAR/KULLU → DEPART
         │ Morning: final Kasol riverside walk.
         │ Buy local Parvati Valley honey.
         │ Bhuntar Airport (30 km) or Kullu town
         │ for bus to Chandigarh/Delhi.

  ─── CHANDRAKHANI QUICK FACTS ───────────────────────────
  Trail start   : Rumsu village (6 km from Naggar)
  Pass altitude : 3,660m
  Total distance: ~28 km full circuit to Jari
  Snow on pass  : Possible May–June (gaiters useful)
  Guide hire    : Naggar village or Manali Mall Road
  Best combo    : Pair with Naggar Castle (Day 1) and
                  Malana village visit (Day 4) for
                  the full cultural-natural circuit
