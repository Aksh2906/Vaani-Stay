PROPERTY 01 — PINE RIDGE HOMESTAY
Location: Old Manali, Manali, Himachal Pradesh — 2,050m
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Pine Ridge Homestay sits on a quiet lane off the Old Manali
road, surrounded by old deodar pines and apple orchards. The
property is a traditional Himachali wooden house, two storeys,
with a slate roof and a wide front veranda that looks out over
the Beas valley. The host family — three generations — lives
on the ground floor and manages everything personally.

ROOMS AVAILABLE
---------------

Room 01 — Valley View Double
Type: Double bed (standard)
Floor: First floor, east-facing
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹1,800/night (off-season Oct–Apr), ₹2,800/night (peak May–Sep)
Bathroom: Attached, with hot water geyser (6 AM–10 AM, 6 PM–10 PM)
View: Direct view of Beas valley and snow peaks
Amenities: Woollen blankets, electric heater, reading lamp, small work desk,
           WiFi (20 Mbps), power backup
Meals: Breakfast and dinner included in room rate

Room 02 — Garden Cottage
Type: Separate cottage, one room with king bed
Floor: Ground level, private entrance
Capacity: 2 guests (extra mattress for 1 child available)
Price: ₹2,500/night (off-season), ₹3,800/night (peak)
Bathroom: Attached, western-style, hot water available all day
View: Apple orchard and garden
Amenities: Sit-out with two chairs, woollen blankets, electric heater,
           WiFi (20 Mbps), power backup, small refrigerator
Meals: Breakfast and dinner included

Room 03 — Dormitory (Backpacker)
Type: Mixed dormitory, 6 bunk beds
Floor: First floor
Capacity: 6 guests (per bed booking)
Price: ₹600/bed/night (year-round)
Bathroom: Shared (2 bathrooms for the dorm floor), hot water 6–10 AM
Amenities: Personal locker, reading light, charging point per bed, WiFi
Meals: Breakfast included per bed

PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------
- Bonfire area in the garden (October–February, complimentary wood provided)
- Outdoor seating with mountain view
- Host prepares traditional Himachali meals on request (Dham, Siddu, Madra)
- Pickup from Manali bus stand: ₹150 flat
- Bicycle rental available: ₹300/day (2 cycles)
- Nearest ATM: 1.2 km (Mall Road)
- Nearest medical: Manali Civil Hospital, 2 km

PROPERTY 02 — TIRTHAN RIVER HOUSE
Location: Gushaini Village, Tirthan Valley, Kullu — 1,700m
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The Tirthan River House is built on the bank of the Tirthan
river, close enough that you can hear the water from every
room. It is a stone-and-timber structure owned by a retired
schoolteacher and his wife who moved back to their ancestral
village in 2019 to open it to guests. There are only three
rooms, kept deliberately small so that the property never
feels crowded. The kitchen is the centre of the house —
guests are welcome to sit at the kitchen table and watch, or
help, with the cooking.

ROOMS AVAILABLE
---------------

Room 01 — Riverside Room
Type: Double bed
Floor: Ground floor, river-facing
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹2,200/night (Oct–Mar), ₹3,200/night (Apr–Sep)
Bathroom: Attached, hot water solar-heated (backup geyser on cloudy days)
View: Direct river view, rear window opens above the water
Amenities: Thick Kullu wool blankets, heater, mosquito net (summer), WiFi
Meals: All three meals included (MAP — breakfast + dinner standard,
       full board available at ₹300 extra per person per day)

Room 02 — Forest-Facing Double
Type: Double bed
Floor: First floor, forest-facing
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹1,900/night (Oct–Mar), ₹2,800/night (Apr–Sep)
Bathroom: Attached, hot water geyser
View: Dense mixed forest up the hillside
Amenities: Kullu wool blankets, heater, WiFi, small balcony
Meals: Breakfast and dinner included

Room 03 — Twin Room
Type: Two single beds
Floor: First floor
Capacity: 2 guests (suitable for friends travelling together)
Price: ₹1,700/night (Oct–Mar), ₹2,600/night (Apr–Sep)
Bathroom: Shared (exclusive to this room — one bathroom per floor)
View: Garden
Amenities: Blankets, heater, WiFi
Meals: Breakfast and dinner included

PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------
- Fishing in the Tirthan river: host arranges permits (₹200/day, GHNP buffer zone)
- The host is a registered GHNP guide and can lead nature walks
- Trout cooked fresh on request — ask the evening before
- GHNP entry gate: 6 km by road (host provides drop)
- No alcohol sold on premises; guests may bring their own
- Children above 5 welcome; cot available for infants at no charge
- Nearest ATM: Banjar town, 12 km

PROPERTY 03 — SPITI STONE HOMESTAY
Location: Kaza Town, Spiti Valley, Lahaul & Spiti — 3,800m
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Spiti Stone Homestay is a traditional flat-roofed stone house
in the centre of Kaza, the administrative headquarters of Spiti
Valley. The house is built in the classic Spitian style — thick
mud-mortar walls that keep out the brutal winter cold, small
deep-set windows, a prayer room on the top floor with butter
lamps that the family lights every evening. The host family has
lived in Kaza for four generations and the grandmother still
speaks only Spitian; her son translates with pride.

ROOMS AVAILABLE
---------------

Room 01 — Standard Double
Type: Double bed (traditional wooden frame, thick yak-wool quilts)
Floor: First floor
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹1,400/night (May–Oct open season only — property closes Nov–Apr)
Bathroom: Attached, hot water from wood-fired boiler (ready 7–9 AM, 7–9 PM)
View: Rooftop access to 360° view of Kaza valley and Key Monastery ridge
Amenities: Yak-wool quilts (rated to -10°C), altitude sickness tablet supply
           (ask host), prayer flags outside window, WiFi (limited, 5 Mbps)
Meals: Breakfast and dinner included (Tibetan bread, butter tea, dal-rice, momos)

Room 02 — Family Room
Type: One double + one single bed
Floor: Second floor
Capacity: 3 guests
Price: ₹2,000/night (May–Oct)
Bathroom: Shared (one clean bathroom per floor), hot water on schedule
View: Kaza village and monastery
Amenities: Yak-wool quilts, electric blanket available on request, WiFi
Meals: All meals included for all guests in this room (full board)

PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------
- Host arranges inner line permits for Kaza (required for foreign nationals)
- Motorbike rental referral: Royal Enfield from town, ₹1,200–1,500/day
- Home-cooked Spitian food: Tsampa, Chhurpi, Butter tea, Thukpa
- Altitude advisory: property is at 3,800m — guests should rest for
  24 hours on arrival, avoid alcohol on first night
- The host's son is a photographer and offers guided photo walks at sunrise
  to Key Monastery (15 km) — ₹800 per group
- Nearest medical: Kaza District Hospital, 0.8 km
- The property closes every year between November and late April due to
  road closures (Rohtang Pass and Kunzum Pass both shut in winter)

PROPERTY 04 — KASAULI COLONIAL BUNGALOW
Location: Upper Mall Road, Kasauli, Solan — 1,795m
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Built in 1923 as a British officer's residence, this bungalow
is one of the original cantonment structures on the Upper Mall.
It has been in the same family since 1962 and opened as a
homestay in 2015. The rooms are large, with high ceilings,
bay windows, original wooden floors, and a wraparound veranda
furnished with old cane chairs. Kasauli Cantonment rules
prohibit motor vehicles on the Upper Mall — the road outside
is for walkers only.

ROOMS AVAILABLE
---------------

Room 01 — Heritage Suite
Type: King bed, heritage furniture (original 1920s wardrobe and writing desk)
Floor: Ground floor
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹3,500/night (weekdays), ₹4,500/night (weekends and holidays)
Bathroom: Attached, cast-iron clawfoot tub + shower, hot water 24 hours
View: Veranda and deodar garden, Chandigarh plains visible on clear days
Amenities: Fireplace (active October–February, firewood provided), WiFi,
           premium linen, electric backup, heritage reading library
Meals: Full English + Indian breakfast included; dinner on request (₹500/person)

Room 02 — Garden Room (Double)
Type: Queen bed
Floor: First floor
Capacity: 2 guests
Price: ₹2,500/night (weekdays), ₹3,200/night (weekends)
Bathroom: Attached, shower, hot water 24 hours
View: Cottage garden and rear orchard
Amenities: WiFi, woollen blankets, electric heater, writing desk

Room 03 — Orchard Room (Twin)

Price: ₹2,200/night (weekdays), ₹2,800/night (weekends)
Bathroom: Attached, shower, hot water

PROPERTY HIGHLIGHTS
-------------------

- Best property in Kasauli for couples and heritage lovers
- Nearest ATM: Kasauli Market, 0.4 km (10-minute walk)
